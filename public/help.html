Shipping is free when the items in your order add up to €70 or more. The shipping cost is always shown before you pay.

We dispatch every order from our own stock in Spain within 3 business days. Parcels are delivered to your address, never to a pick-up point. When your order ships, you get an email with the tracking link.

We don't ship to other countries yet.

Returns

You have 14 days from the day you receive your order to return it, without giving a reason.

  1. Email hola@pursec.club with your order email and the pieces you want to return. You can use the model withdrawal form, but you don't have to.
  2. We reply with the return address. Send the pieces back unworn, unwashed and with their labels within 14 days of telling us.
  3. We refund the price of the pieces and the standard shipping of the original order within 14 days, to the same payment method.

A piece arrived damaged or you got the wrong one? Write to us within 14 days with a photo and we replace it at no cost.

How to measure

Lay a piece you already own flat. Chest: armpit to armpit. Length: from the highest point of the shoulder to the hem. Sleeve: from the shoulder seam to the cuff.

Payment

Payment is processed by Stripe on its secure checkout. We never see or store your card details. Prices are in euros and include VAT. Your order is confirmed when the payment goes through, and you get the confirmation by email.

FAQ

When does Drop 01 open?
The date isn't public yet. Join the list: you'll get one email before it opens.
Will sold-out pieces come back?
If a size sells out, use "Notify me" on the piece and we'll email you if it comes back.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Write to hola@pursec.club as soon as possible. If it hasn't shipped yet, we change or cancel it. If it has, you can return it within 14 days of receiving it.
A size sold out after I paid. What happens?
It's very rare. If it happens, we email you and refund that piece in full.
Do you ship outside these five countries?
Not yet. For now we ship to Spain, France, Germany, Italy and Portugal.
Where are you based?
In Spain. Every order ships from our own stock here.
